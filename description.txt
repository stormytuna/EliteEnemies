Elite Enemies adds elite variations to enemies and (optionally) bosses! Each variation increases the power of the enemy, but also makes it drop more gold and loot.

- Adds 37 variations that can apply to (almost) any enemy
- Variations range from stat increases to unique effects
- Variations have rarities, which influence how often they appear and how much extra loot they drop
- Adds a new option at world creation to select how abundant you'd like the elites to be. If you change your mind, purchase an Elite Abundancy Globe from the Zoologist
- Multiplayer is supported
- Currently supports English, (partial) Polish and (partial) Spanish, see below if you'd like to contribute another translation
